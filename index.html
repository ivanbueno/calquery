Understand Court Procedures Without the Confusion

How CalQuery helps

CalQuery is an AI assistant that helps you understand court processes, legal forms, and procedural steps. It connects your question to official court information and trusted self-help resources so you can understand what to do next.

Whether you're filing a case, responding to court papers, preparing for a hearing, or learning about legal procedures, CalQuery helps you find reliable information quickly.

Common topics
  • Court procedures and timelines
  • Legal forms and filing steps
  • Civil and criminal court processes
  • Evidence and court hearings
  • Dispute resolution options
  • Self-representation guidance

How it works
  • Retrieval-augmented generation: each query first retrieves relevant passages from indexed documents, then generates an answer grounded in that evidence.
  • Single-index partitioning: documents carry a site label, so one shared index can be filtered by source site when needed.
  • Multi-index routing: a routing layer can split broad questions into targeted sub-queries across multiple indexes and combine the results.
  • Hallucination reduction: answers are constrained to retrieved context, include source citations, and return an insufficient-information response when evidence is missing.

CalQuery provides general legal information, not legal advice. Always verify information with official court sources or a qualified attorney.
